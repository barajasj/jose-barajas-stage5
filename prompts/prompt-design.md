# Prompt Design

## Requirement Interpreter

Purpose:
Convert HVAC tickets into structured implementation guidance.

Primary Focus:
- HVAC terminology
- Functional requirements
- Ambiguities

---

## Adversarial Reviewer

Purpose:
Challenge assumptions and detect missing requirements.

Primary Focus:
- Missing behavior
- Unsupported conclusions
- Risk discovery

---

## Final Review Writer

Purpose:
Generate stakeholder-facing synthesis.

Primary Focus:
- Clarity
- Accuracy
- Completeness
